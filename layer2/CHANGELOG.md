    timeout, and a plain "AI service busy" answer (details in the audit log) instead of raw errors.
- Metric lane: an LLM planner that may only pick from the menu (metric, breakdowns, filters, real values) when the
  rules found no metric or ignored something the question names; "never silently wrong": what could not be applied
